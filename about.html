Our story

A small salon with a clear point of view

Lumen Lane opened on a quiet downtown block so York guests could have unhurried color and cuts without the city commute.

Why we opened

Soft light, fewer chairs, better conversations

We keep the book intentionally light. That means your stylist has time to listen, formulate carefully, and finish your hair so it still looks like you on a Tuesday morning.

This is a fictional sample site for a York, PA salon concept - useful for demos and portfolio feedback, not a live booking business.

Consult first

Every color visit starts with goals, lifestyle, and how often you want to maintain.

Healthy canvas

We prioritize bond care and gentle processing over dramatic one-day damage.

Local and personal

York neighbors, downtown parking nearby, and a studio that feels like a living room - not a factory floor.
